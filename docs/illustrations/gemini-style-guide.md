# BackRight stretch illustrations: style guide and prompts for Gemini

How to use this:

1. Open a new Gemini chat (image generation on).
2. Paste the **master style block** once, on its own, and say "Remember this style for the whole conversation."
3. Generate the two test stretches below (1 and 5). Fix the style with follow-ups ("make the head smaller", "no face") until you like one.
4. **Upload the image you approved as a reference** and say "Same figure, outfit, colours and style as this image" for every other stretch. This is what keeps all 17 consistent.
5. Save each result as `<id>.png` (ids are in brackets below, e.g. `neck-tilt.png`) into one folder and tell me where it is. I'll resize them, wire them into the Move card and guided routine, and add alt text.

Tips: ask for a 4:3 landscape image. If a limb comes out wrong, say exactly what is wrong ("the right arm should go behind the head, not in front") instead of regenerating from scratch. Left and right are from the *viewer's* point of view in these prompts.

## Master style block (paste first)

```
Create a set of flat vector-style illustrations for a posture and movement app, in the style of modern "office worker stretching at a desk" stock illustration sets: friendly flat characters in a cosy, lightly furnished workspace. Rules that apply to every image:

CHARACTER: one adult, the same person in every image. Friendly flat-vector character with a small simple face (two dot eyes, tiny nose, a small calm smile), dark brown shoulder-length hair tied back in a low ponytail (visible from both side and front), natural human proportions with a smallish head, tapering limbs, simple hands with fingers hinted and simple shoes. Medium build. No outlines; shapes are filled with flat colour, with one slightly darker tone per shape for depth (under the chin, inside the elbow, the far arm and leg).

CLOTHING (identical in every image): mustard-orange long-sleeve top (#E9A23B), dark navy trousers (#2F3A5A), white sneakers, warm light skin tone (#F0C7A8), hair (#3B2F2F).

SCENE: a calm, uncluttered home-office or office. Pale blob-shaped colour patch behind the figure in a soft lilac-grey (#E7E6F2). Simple furniture in muted tones: desk and shelf in grey-blue (#8C98A3), chair in terracotta (#C65D4B). Props repeat across images: a laptop, a desk lamp, a small potted plant with orange leaves, one or two books. Thin light-grey floor shadow under the figure. The stretch itself must be the focus; keep props small and out of the way.

GUIDANCE (subtle): the stretched muscle area gets a soft translucent coral tint (#F29B8C) over the body. Optionally, one small curved arrow in dark navy (#2F3A5A) showing the direction of movement. At most one tint and one arrow per image.

STYLE: modern, calm, flat colour. No gradients, no drop shadows, no textures, no 3D, no cartoon exaggeration.

NEVER: text, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, extra limbs, extra fingers, a different outfit, hair or colours between images.

Output: landscape 4:3.
```

Tip: first screenshot one or two of the search-result images you like and attach them to the first Gemini message as a *style reference only*. Say: "Use these only as a loose reference for the flat character style and calm mood. Create a new original character and scene; don't copy any specific image." Don't use or ship the reference images themselves.

## Prompts per stretch

Each prompt is pasted after the master block (or with the approved image attached). Where a prompt says "orange arrow" or "coral glow", use the master block's small navy arrow and soft coral tint. Seated stretches happen at the desk with the terracotta chair and props; standing ones in the same room, with the desk edge visible when the prompt mentions it.

### 1. Neck side tilt [neck-tilt] (test this one)
```
Front view of the figure sitting upright on a simple chair, shoulders relaxed and level, hands resting on the thighs. The head is tilted so the ear moves toward the viewer's left shoulder (the shoulder itself does not rise). One small curved arrow above the head shows the tilt. Soft coral tint along the right side of the neck (the viewer's right), the side being stretched.
```

### 2. Chin tucks [chin-tuck]
```
Side view, figure sitting upright facing right, eyes level. The head has glided straight back, the chin level (not tipped down). A faint ghost outline shows where the head was before, slightly forward. One short orange horizontal arrow points backward from the chin. Coral glow on the back of the neck.
```

### 3. Shoulder rolls [shoulder-rolls]
```
Front view, figure sitting upright, shoulders lifted toward the ears, arms relaxed at the sides. A circular orange arrow around each shoulder showing a backward roll. Coral glow on both shoulders and upper trapezius.
```

### 4. Seated chest opener [seated-chest-opener]
```
Side view, figure sitting tall near the front of a chair, both hands behind the head, elbows drawn wide and back, chest lifted, lower back not arched. One orange arrow pointing back at the elbow. Coral glow across the front of the chest.
```

### 5. Seated spinal twist [seated-twist] (test this one)
```
Three-quarter front view, figure sitting tall on a chair with both feet flat. The viewer's-left hand rests on the opposite knee, the other hand holds the chair back behind. The torso is turned and the head looks over the shoulder. One curved orange arrow wraps around the torso showing the rotation. Coral glow on the middle of the back/side of the waist.
```

### 6. Wrist and forearm stretch [wrist-stretch]
```
Side view, figure sitting upright at a desk. One arm is extended forward at chest height, palm facing up, fingers pointing down; the other hand gently pulls the fingers back. One small orange arrow on the pulling hand. Coral glow along the underside of the extended forearm.
```

### 7. Seated figure-four [figure-four]
```
Side view, figure sitting on a chair, one ankle crossed over the opposite knee (a figure-four shape), back long, torso leaning slightly forward. One orange arrow showing the forward lean from the chest. Coral glow on the hip and glute of the crossed leg.
```

### 8. Upper-back reach [upper-back-reach]
```
Side view, figure sitting, fingers interlaced and palms pushed away at chest height, upper back rounded, head dropped between the arms. One orange arrow pushing forward from the hands. Coral glow between the shoulder blades.
```

### 9. 20-20-20 eye break [eye-break]
```
Side view, figure sitting at a desk with a laptop, next to a large window; the figure looks out through the window at distant hills and a tree far away. A thin dotted orange line runs from the eyes to the distance. No arrows. Soft coral glow around the eyes.
```

### 10. Behind-the-back chest opener [behind-back-opener]
```
Side view, figure standing tall, feet hip-width apart, hands clasped behind the back with straight arms lifted slightly, chest lifted, ribs down. One orange arrow showing the hands drawing back and down. Coral glow on the chest and front of the shoulders.
```

### 11. Hip-flexor lunge [hip-flexor]
```
Side view, figure standing in a short lunge: one foot forward with the front knee over the ankle, the other leg stretched back with the heel lifted. One hand rests on the edge of a desk for balance. Pelvis tucked, hips drifting slightly forward. One orange arrow at the hips pointing forward. Coral glow on the front of the back leg's hip.
```

### 12. Calf raises [calf-raises]
```
Side view, figure standing with one hand on the edge of a desk, risen onto the toes with heels lifted. One vertical orange arrow pointing up at the heels. Coral glow on the calves.
```

### 13. Standing side bend [side-bend]
```
Front view, figure standing with feet hip-width apart, one arm reaching overhead and leaning gently to the opposite side, both hips facing forward. One curved orange arrow along the lean. Coral glow along the stretched side of the body from hip to armpit.
```

### 14. Soft forward fold [forward-fold]
```
Side view, figure standing with soft knees, hinged forward at the hips, head and arms hanging loose toward the floor. One orange arrow pointing down along the arms. Coral glow on the backs of the legs and lower back.
```

### 15. Wall angels [wall-angels]
```
Front view, figure standing with their back against a pale wall, feet a few inches out, both arms in a goalpost shape (elbows bent at 90 degrees, upper arms level with the shoulders) touching the wall. Two short orange arrows (up and down) beside the arms. Coral glow on the upper back and shoulders.
```

### 16. March in place [march]
```
Side view, figure marching on the spot: one knee raised comfortably high, the opposite arm swinging forward and the other back. Two small orange motion arcs near the knee and hand. No glow.
```

### 17. Walk and refill your water [walk-water]
```
Side view, figure walking, holding a water bottle in one hand. Behind them a simple water cooler or sink in muted grey-blue. Small orange footstep marks or one short forward arrow. No glow.
```

## Hand-off checklist (for me, Claude)

- Files: 17 PNGs named by id. Aim for about 1200 px wide, under 150 KB each after compression.
- In the app they appear in the Move card (expanded stretch) and the guided routine. The page background can be light or dark, so a rounded card background will be added behind each image.
- Alt text comes from `stretches.js` (name plus first step).

## Status

Done. All 17 stretch images and the 10 desk-setup guide images were generated in one Gemini chat with the master block
above and are bundled as WebP in `src/renderer/img/stretch/<id>.webp` and `src/renderer/img/guide/<sit|stand>-<key>.webp`.
Alt text lives in `src/renderer/guide-art.js` and in the stretch names. To redo one, regenerate it in the same chat, save it
as PNG, convert with `cwebp -q 84 -m 6 in.png -o out.webp`, and drop it over the existing file.
