## BackRight 0.2.0 — history, movement breaks and a stretch library

0.1.0 told you when you slipped. 0.2.0 helps you see whether you're improving, and nudges you to get up and move. Still no images stored, still no network.

### What's new
- **Progress tab.** How much of today you held good posture, how many times you slipped, and breaks taken. A plain-language answer to "is it getting better?" compares the last 7 days with the 7 before, with a 14/30-day chart. If you changed the sensitivity in that period the chart says so, because it changes what counts as a slip. **Export as CSV** or **Delete history** any time.
- **Movement-break reminders.** After 30 minutes of desk time (time you're present and being monitored), a small green pill asks if you'd like a quick stretch: *Show me*, *Snooze 10 min* or *Skip*. It never takes focus, waits behind a live posture nudge, respects Snooze, and stepping away for 5 minutes counts as a break automatically. Interval (20/30/45/60 min) or off is in the Settings tab. Also in the menu-bar menu: *Take a movement break…*.
- **Stretch library and Move card.** 16 easy stretches for sitting at your desk or standing up (including a 20-20-20 eye break), shown on the Monitor tab with a guided 2-minute routine that steps through three stretches with a timer.
- **Sitting, standing or both.** The first screen now has checkboxes, and setup follows them: one mode means one calibration and no Sitting / Standing switch; both means each is calibrated in turn. Change it later in Settings → How you work. Existing users keep both.
- **A "Today" line** on the Monitor tab and a refreshed first-run summary.

### Privacy
The app now keeps one more small file next to your settings: `history.json`, daily totals only (minutes monitored, minutes slipping, slips, breaks, sensitivity). Numbers only; no images, ever. All disk writes go through one module and a test enforces that. The Privacy tab and the first-run walkthrough say so.

Install steps are unchanged from 0.1.0 (the app is still not notarized): open BackRight, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Your calibration and settings carry over if you replace the old app.

### Notes
- Movement breaks are **on by default** (every 30 minutes); turn them off in the Settings tab.
- The stretches are general suggestions, not medical advice; stop if anything hurts.
- Requires macOS 13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.
