Concept demo. A mock-up of BackRight as an app a physio prescribes. The patient, physio and all numbers are made up. This is not part of the BackRight app and not medical advice.

What this shows: the patient does and ticks off the prescribed exercises, logs pain and what they did that day, and BackRight adds the posture time it measured on-device. The physio edits the program (swap, add, change sets and reps), publishes it, and sees how adherence, posture and pain moved before and after each change.

What it can't show: a before/after difference is not proof the change caused it. Sam's other activities, rest and plain recovery all move these numbers. The dashboard is a conversation aid for the physio, not a verdict.
